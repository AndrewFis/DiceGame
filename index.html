How to play
  1. Flip the coin once. That color starts Round 1; starters alternate after that.
  2. Both colors roll five dice once. Values stay fixed for the whole round.
  3. Tap or drag one active die, then choose an open square. Players alternate colors.
  4. Every completed row, column, or diagonal scores one point: odd sums for Black, even sums for White.
  5. All eight lines score when the grid is full. The second player keeps one unplayed die.
